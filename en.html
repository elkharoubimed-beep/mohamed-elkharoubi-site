Profile

Mohamed Elkharoubi is a teacher and head of the Barrage Al Wahda School Group in Lamjaara, Ouezzane Province. He is a member of the Lamjaara municipal council, chairs the committee allocated to the opposition on the council, and is a community and union activist interested in local affairs and residents’ concerns.

He previously served as local secretary of the National Education Union (FDT) in Lamjaara and was a member of its provincial and regional offices. He is a member of the union’s national council and of the national council of the Socialist Union of Popular Forces. He also previously chaired the Provincial Federation of Mothers’, Fathers’ and Guardians’ Associations in Ouezzane Province.

Alongside his educational and civic work, he develops mobile applications and works professionally in graphic design.

Educational and digital achievements

First national prize in WikiChallenge

Mohamed Elkharoubi coordinated the participation of the Mohamed Abdou School Group in Ouezzane Province in the WikiChallenge writing competition. The school won the first national prize for 2023 for an article about Ouezzane produced by sixth-grade pupils from the Aïn El Hafa school unit.

He attended the award ceremony with the provincial director of National Education in Ouezzane and the school principal on 18 May 2024, during the 29th Rabat International Publishing and Book Fair.

Regional Super Codeur programming hackathon

Mohamed Elkharoubi represented the Ouezzane Provincial Directorate at the regional Super Codeur programming hackathon, organised by the Ministry of National Education, Preschool and Sports in partnership with Orange. He received a participation certificate recognising his active contribution to this digital education event.

Africa Code Week 2020 mentoring

Mohamed Elkharoubi received a certificate of recognition for his work training and mentoring pupils who participated in Africa Code Week 2020, held from 2 to 29 November 2020. This participation contributed to the Ouezzane Provincial Directorate achieving an honourable ranking at regional level.

Professional training in graphic design and video editing

In 2022, Mohamed Elkharoubi obtained two online training certificates from Easyno LTD Academy in graphic design and video editing. The training covered Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Illustrator, and professional use of Adobe software for graphic design and video editing.

Graphic design recognition certificate

The Ouezzane Provincial Directorate of the Ministry of National Education, Preschool and Sports awarded Mohamed Elkharoubi a certificate of thanks and recognition for his graphic design efforts and contribution to educational activities.
